About me

A developer who also runs a store

Why my websites are built for real businesses, not just for show.

I'm Umar Hayat, a WordPress, WooCommerce and Shopify developer based in Lahore, Pakistan. I build and run online stores and business websites, from product catalogue and checkout to security and marketing.

What makes my work practical is that I run one myself: Comfort House, a live WooCommerce store selling bedsheets and comforter sets across Pakistan. I handle the catalogue, checkout, shipping, ads and day-to-day operations, so I build websites the way a business owner needs them to work.

I keep things simple and reliable: fast, mobile-friendly pages, a secure WordPress setup, and tracking that shows what your marketing is actually doing.
